📘 Concept Notes

01

Public Finance

Public finance explains how government raises resources, spends them, borrows when necessary and transfers resources among Union, States and local governments.

02

Fiscal Policy

Fiscal policy covers taxation, public expenditure and government borrowing. It can influence growth, employment, aggregate demand, inflation and distribution. Monetary policy is different and is conducted by RBI.

03

Government Receipts

Receipts are broadly Revenue Receipts and Capital Receipts. Revenue receipts normally do not create a liability or reduce an asset. Capital receipts either create a liability or reduce an asset.

04

Revenue Receipts

Revenue receipts are current/recurring receipts. Main components: Tax Revenue and Non-Tax Revenue. Examples include income tax, GST, customs, interest, dividends, fees and fines.

05

Tax Revenue

Tax revenue is compulsory revenue collected under legal authority. Taxes can be classified as direct/indirect and by the government level imposing them.

06

Non-Tax Revenue

Includes interest received by government, dividends/profits from government enterprises, fees, fines, penalties, licence fees, royalties and certain service receipts.

07

Capital Receipts

Includes borrowings, recovery of loans and other non-debt capital receipts such as specified disinvestment/asset-sale receipts. Borrowing creates a liability; loan recovery reduces a financial asset.

08

Debt vs Non-Debt Capital Receipts

Debt receipts create liabilities: market borrowing, treasury bills, external borrowing. Non-debt capital receipts do not create a new debt liability: loan recovery and certain miscellaneous capital receipts.

09

Government Expenditure

Expenditure is broadly Revenue Expenditure and Capital Expenditure. Revenue expenditure generally meets recurring obligations/maintains existing capacity. Capital expenditure creates or acquires assets, reduces liabilities or supports productive capacity.

10

Revenue Expenditure

Examples: salaries, pensions, interest payments, subsidies, routine administration and grants that do not create capital assets. High revenue expenditure is not automatically bad; purpose and outcomes matter.

11

Capital Expenditure

Examples: roads, railways, defence equipment, public buildings, capital projects and certain loans to States for capital investment. It can expand productive capacity.

12

Effective Capital Expenditure

Effective Capital Expenditure = Government capital expenditure + grants-in-aid for creation of capital assets.

13

Direct Taxes

Economic burden normally falls on the person/entity on whom the tax is imposed. Examples: personal income tax, corporation tax, capital-gains taxation and securities transaction tax. Wealth tax is historical; it was abolished from 2015.

14

Indirect Taxes

Imposed on transactions, supply, production, imports or consumption; burden can generally be passed through prices. GST is the major broad-based indirect tax. Customs and specified central excise remain important.

15

GST

GST is a destination-based tax on supply of goods and services. It was rolled out nationally on 1 July 2017 after the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016. Intra-State: CGST + SGST/UTGST. Inter-State: IGST.

16

Taxes Outside GST

GST did not replace every tax. Customs duties, stamp duty, electricity duty, tax on alcoholic liquor for human consumption and specified petroleum-related taxes remain outside GST. Property and motor-vehicle taxes also remain outside GST.

17

Cess and Surcharge

Surcharge is an additional charge on a tax. Cess is earmarked for a specified purpose. Union surcharge and cess are generally outside the divisible pool used for tax devolution.

18

Revenue Deficit

Revenue Deficit = Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipts. It shows a gap on the revenue account.

19

Fiscal Deficit

Fiscal Deficit = Total Expenditure − Total Receipts excluding borrowings. It broadly indicates government borrowing requirement.

20

Primary Deficit

Primary Deficit = Fiscal Deficit − Interest Payments. It shows the fiscal gap after removing interest payments.

21

Effective Revenue Deficit

Effective Revenue Deficit = Revenue Deficit − Grants for creation of capital assets.

22

Annual Financial Statement

Article 112 provides for the Union Annual Financial Statement. It presents estimated receipts and expenditure for the financial year.

23

Consolidated Fund / Contingency Fund / Public Account

Consolidated Fund is the main government fund. Contingency Fund is for unforeseen urgent expenditure under constitutional/legal procedure. Public Account contains money held by government in a fiduciary capacity, such as provident funds and deposits.

24

Finance Commission

Finance Commission is a constitutional body under Article 280. It is normally constituted every five years or earlier. It recommends distribution of net Union tax proceeds between Union and States, grants-in-aid principles and related fiscal matters.

25

GST Council

Article 279A provides for the GST Council. It recommends GST rates, exemptions, model laws and principles concerning place of supply and other GST matters.

26

Important Articles

Article 265: no tax without authority of law. Article 112: Annual Financial Statement. Article 246A: GST legislative power. Article 269A: inter-State GST. Article 279A: GST Council. Article 280: Finance Commission. Article 276: profession/trade/calling/employment tax.

27

Budget 2026-27: Core Numbers

BE 2026-27: total expenditure about ₹53.47 lakh crore; non-debt receipts ₹36.5 lakh crore; Centre net tax receipts about ₹28.7 lakh crore; gross tax revenue ₹44.04 lakh crore; revenue receipts ₹35.33 lakh crore; capital expenditure ₹12.22 lakh crore; effective capital expenditure ₹17.15 lakh crore; revenue expenditure ₹41.25 lakh crore.

28

Budget 2026-27: Deficit & Borrowing

Fiscal deficit target is 4.3% of GDP. Net market borrowing from dated securities is estimated at ₹11.7 lakh crore and gross market borrowing at ₹17.2 lakh crore. Interest payments are estimated at ₹14.04 lakh crore.

29

Budget 2026-27: Finance Commission

Budget 2026-27 is the first year of the Sixteenth Finance Commission award period. The government accepted retaining States' share at 41% of the divisible pool.

30

Easy Deficit Example

If revenue receipts = ₹100, revenue expenditure = ₹120, total expenditure = ₹180, and receipts excluding borrowings = ₹140: Revenue deficit = ₹20; fiscal deficit = ₹40. If interest payments = ₹15, primary deficit = ₹25.

🎯 Last-Minute Revision

  • Receipts: Revenue = normally no new liability/no asset reduction; Capital = liability creation or asset reduction.
  • Expenditure: Revenue = recurring/maintenance; Capital = asset creation/acquisition or liability reduction.
  • 265 = authority of law; 112 = Annual Financial Statement; 280 = Finance Commission; 279A = GST Council; 246A = GST.
  • Budget 2026-27: FD 4.3% GDP; Capex ₹12.22 lakh crore; effective Capex ₹17.15 lakh crore; total expenditure ₹53.47 lakh crore.

📘 Concept Notes

01

Public Finance

সরকার কীভাবে অর্থ সংগ্রহ করে, ব্যয় করে, প্রয়োজনে ঋণ নেয় এবং Union–State–Local স্তরে সম্পদ বণ্টন করে—তার সামগ্রিক ব্যবস্থাই Public Finance।

02

Fiscal Policy

Taxation, public expenditure এবং borrowing-এর নীতিকে Fiscal Policy বলা হয়। এটি growth, employment, demand, inflation ও distribution-কে প্রভাবিত করতে পারে। Monetary Policy আলাদা এবং RBI-এর সঙ্গে যুক্ত।

03

Government Receipts

সরকারি প্রাপ্তি প্রধানত Revenue Receipts ও Capital Receipts। Revenue Receipt সাধারণত নতুন liability তৈরি করে না বা asset কমায় না। Capital Receipt liability তৈরি করে বা asset কমায়।

04

Revenue Receipts

চলতি/recurring receipt। প্রধান দুই অংশ Tax Revenue ও Non-Tax Revenue। Income tax, GST, customs, interest, dividend, fees, fines ইত্যাদি উদাহরণ।

05

Tax Revenue

আইনের authority-এর অধীনে বাধ্যতামূলকভাবে সংগৃহীত রাজস্ব। Direct/Indirect এবং Union/State/Local স্তর অনুযায়ী শ্রেণিবিন্যাস করা যায়।

06

Non-Tax Revenue

Government-এর interest, government enterprises-এর dividend/profit, fees, fines, penalties, licence fees, royalties এবং কিছু service receipt এর মধ্যে পড়ে।

07

Capital Receipts

Borrowing, loan recovery এবং কিছু non-debt capital receipt যেমন নির্দিষ্ট disinvestment/asset-sale receipt। Borrowing liability সৃষ্টি করে; loan recovery financial asset কমায়।

08

Debt বনাম Non-Debt Capital Receipt

Debt receipt liability বাড়ায়—market borrowing, treasury bills, external borrowing। Non-debt receipt নতুন debt liability তৈরি করে না—যেমন loan recovery।

09

Government Expenditure

Expenditure মূলত Revenue ও Capital। Revenue expenditure recurring obligation/maintenance-এর সঙ্গে যুক্ত। Capital expenditure asset তৈরি/অর্জন করে, liability কমায় বা productive capacity বাড়ায়।

10

Revenue Expenditure

Salary, pension, interest, subsidy, routine administration এবং capital asset তৈরি না-করা grant-এর মতো ব্যয়। বেশি হলেই খারাপ—এমন নয়; উদ্দেশ্য গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।

11

Capital Expenditure

Road, railway, defence equipment, public building, capital project এবং capital investment-এর জন্য কিছু State loan-এর মতো ব্যয়। এটি productive capacity বাড়াতে পারে।

12

Effective Capital Expenditure

Effective Capital Expenditure = Government capital expenditure + capital asset তৈরির জন্য grants-in-aid।

13

Direct Tax

যার উপর legal tax liability পড়ে, সাধারণত তিনিই economic burden বহন করেন। Income tax, corporation tax, capital-gains tax, STT উদাহরণ। Wealth Tax 2015 থেকে বিলুপ্ত।

14

Indirect Tax

Transaction/supply/production/import/consumption-এর উপর tax; burden দামের মাধ্যমে স্থানান্তর হতে পারে। GST প্রধান indirect tax। Customs ও নির্দিষ্ট central excise গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।

15

GST

Goods ও services-এর supply-এর উপর destination-based tax। 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016-এর পরে 1 July 2017 থেকে চালু। Intra-State → CGST + SGST/UTGST; Inter-State → IGST।

16

GST-এর বাইরে

Customs duty, stamp duty, electricity duty, alcoholic liquor for human consumption-এর tax, নির্দিষ্ট petroleum-related taxes GST-এর বাইরে। Property tax ও motor-vehicle tax-ও বাইরে।

17

Cess ও Surcharge

Surcharge হল tax-এর উপর অতিরিক্ত charge। Cess নির্দিষ্ট উদ্দেশ্যে earmarked levy। Union cess/surcharge সাধারণত divisible pool-এর বাইরে।

18

Revenue Deficit

Revenue Deficit = Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipts। এটি revenue account-এর gap।

19

Fiscal Deficit

Fiscal Deficit = Total Expenditure − Total Receipts excluding borrowings। এটি সরকারের borrowing requirement-এর গুরুত্বপূর্ণ সূচক।

20

Primary Deficit

Primary Deficit = Fiscal Deficit − Interest Payments।

21

Effective Revenue Deficit

Effective Revenue Deficit = Revenue Deficit − capital asset তৈরির grants।

22

Annual Financial Statement

Article 112 Union Annual Financial Statement-এর কথা বলে। একটি financial year-এর estimated receipts ও expenditure এখানে দেখানো হয়।

23

Consolidated Fund / Contingency Fund / Public Account

Consolidated Fund প্রধান government fund। Contingency Fund জরুরি unforeseen expenditure-এর জন্য। Public Account-এ সরকার fiduciary capacity-তে provident fund ও deposits-এর মতো অর্থ রাখে।

24

Finance Commission

Article 280-এর Finance Commission একটি constitutional body। সাধারণত প্রতি পাঁচ বছর অন্তর বা আগে গঠিত হয়। Union taxes-এর net proceeds বণ্টন ও grants-in-aid-এর নীতি নিয়ে সুপারিশ করে।

25

GST Council

Article 279A GST Council-এর সাংবিধানিক ভিত্তি। GST rates, exemptions, model laws এবং অন্যান্য গুরুত্বপূর্ণ বিষয় নিয়ে recommendation দেয়।

26

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ Articles

265 = authority of law ছাড়া tax নয়। 112 = Annual Financial Statement। 246A = GST। 269A = inter-State GST। 279A = GST Council। 280 = Finance Commission। 276 = profession/trade/calling/employment tax।

27

Budget 2026-27: মূল সংখ্যা

BE 2026-27-এ total expenditure প্রায় ₹53.47 lakh crore; non-debt receipts ₹36.5 lakh crore; net tax receipts প্রায় ₹28.7 lakh crore; gross tax revenue ₹44.04 lakh crore; revenue receipts ₹35.33 lakh crore; capital expenditure ₹12.22 lakh crore; effective capital expenditure ₹17.15 lakh crore; revenue expenditure ₹41.25 lakh crore।

28

Budget 2026-27: Deficit ও Borrowing

Fiscal deficit target 4.3% GDP। Dated securities-এর net market borrowing ₹11.7 lakh crore; gross market borrowing ₹17.2 lakh crore; interest payments ₹14.04 lakh crore।

29

Budget 2026-27: Finance Commission

Budget 2026-27 Sixteenth Finance Commission award period-এর প্রথম বছর। Divisible pool-এ States-এর share 41% রাখার recommendation সরকার গ্রহণ করেছে।

30

সহজ Deficit Example

Revenue receipts ₹100, revenue expenditure ₹120, total expenditure ₹180, borrowings বাদে receipts ₹140 হলে: Revenue deficit ₹20; fiscal deficit ₹40; interest ₹15 হলে primary deficit ₹25।

  • Expenditure: Revenue = recurring/maintenance; Capital = asset creation/acquisition বা liability reduction।

📘 Concept Notes

01

Public Finance

सरकार धन कैसे जुटाती है, खर्च करती है, जरूरत पर उधार लेती है और Union–State–Local स्तरों के बीच संसाधन बांटती है—इसी व्यवस्था को Public Finance समझा जाता है।

02

Fiscal Policy

Taxation, public expenditure और borrowing से जुड़ी सरकारी नीति Fiscal Policy है। यह growth, employment, demand, inflation और distribution को प्रभावित कर सकती है। Monetary Policy अलग है और RBI से जुड़ी है।

03

Government Receipts

सरकारी प्राप्तियां मुख्यतः Revenue Receipts और Capital Receipts हैं। Revenue Receipt सामान्यतः नई liability नहीं बनाती और asset कम नहीं करती। Capital Receipt liability बनाती है या asset घटाती है।

04

Revenue Receipts

Current/recurring receipts। दो प्रमुख भाग Tax Revenue और Non-Tax Revenue। Income tax, GST, customs, interest, dividend, fees और fines उदाहरण हैं।

05

Tax Revenue

कानून के अधिकार के तहत अनिवार्य रूप से वसूला गया राजस्व। Taxes को direct/indirect तथा Union/State/Local स्तर के अनुसार समझा जा सकता है।

06

Non-Tax Revenue

Government interest, government enterprises के dividend/profit, fees, fines, penalties, licence fees, royalties और कुछ service receipts इसमें आते हैं।

07

Capital Receipts

Borrowing, recovery of loans और कुछ non-debt capital receipts जैसे specified disinvestment/asset-sale receipts। Borrowing liability बनाता है; loan recovery financial asset घटाता है।

08

Debt बनाम Non-Debt Capital Receipt

Debt receipt liability बढ़ाती है—market borrowing, treasury bills, external borrowing। Non-debt receipt नई debt liability नहीं बनाती—जैसे loan recovery।

09

Government Expenditure

Expenditure मुख्यतः Revenue और Capital है। Revenue expenditure recurring obligations/maintenance से जुड़ा है। Capital expenditure assets बनाता/खरीदता है, liabilities घटाता है या productive capacity बढ़ाता है।

10

Revenue Expenditure

Salary, pension, interest, subsidy, routine administration और capital asset न बनाने वाले grants उदाहरण हैं। अधिक revenue expenditure अपने-आप में खराब नहीं है।

11

Capital Expenditure

Road, railway, defence equipment, public buildings, capital projects और capital investment के लिए कुछ State loans उदाहरण हैं। यह productive capacity बढ़ा सकता है।

12

Effective Capital Expenditure

Effective Capital Expenditure = Government capital expenditure + capital assets बनाने के लिए grants-in-aid।

13

Direct Tax

जिस पर legal tax liability है, सामान्यतः वही economic burden वहन करता है। Income tax, corporation tax, capital-gains tax और STT उदाहरण हैं। Wealth Tax 2015 से समाप्त है।

14

Indirect Tax

Transaction/supply/production/import/consumption पर tax; burden कीमतों के माध्यम से आगे डाला जा सकता है। GST प्रमुख indirect tax है। Customs और specified central excise भी महत्वपूर्ण हैं।

15

GST

Goods और services की supply पर destination-based tax। 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016 के बाद 1 July 2017 से लागू। Intra-State → CGST + SGST/UTGST; Inter-State → IGST।

16

GST से बाहर

Customs duty, stamp duty, electricity duty, alcoholic liquor for human consumption पर tax और कुछ petroleum-related taxes GST से बाहर हैं। Property और motor-vehicle taxes भी बाहर हैं।

17

Cess और Surcharge

Surcharge किसी tax पर अतिरिक्त charge है। Cess किसी विशेष उद्देश्य के लिए earmarked levy है। Union cess/surcharge सामान्यतः divisible pool से बाहर रहते हैं।

18

Revenue Deficit

Revenue Deficit = Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipts। यह revenue account का gap है।

19

Fiscal Deficit

Fiscal Deficit = Total Expenditure − Total Receipts excluding borrowings। यह borrowing requirement का महत्वपूर्ण संकेतक है।

20

Primary Deficit

Primary Deficit = Fiscal Deficit − Interest Payments।

21

Effective Revenue Deficit

Effective Revenue Deficit = Revenue Deficit − capital asset creation grants।

22

Annual Financial Statement

Article 112 Union Annual Financial Statement से संबंधित है। इसमें financial year की estimated receipts और expenditure होती हैं।

23

Consolidated Fund / Contingency Fund / Public Account

Consolidated Fund मुख्य government fund है। Contingency Fund unforeseen urgent expenditure के लिए है। Public Account में government fiduciary capacity में provident funds और deposits जैसे funds रखती है।

24

Finance Commission

Article 280 के तहत Finance Commission constitutional body है। सामान्यतः हर पांच वर्ष या उससे पहले गठित होती है। Union taxes के net proceeds के वितरण और grants-in-aid से जुड़े सिद्धांतों पर सिफारिश करती है।

25

GST Council

Article 279A GST Council का constitutional आधार है। GST rates, exemptions, model laws और अन्य महत्वपूर्ण GST मामलों पर recommendations देता है।

26

महत्वपूर्ण Articles

265 = authority of law के बिना tax नहीं। 112 = Annual Financial Statement। 246A = GST। 269A = inter-State GST। 279A = GST Council। 280 = Finance Commission। 276 = profession/trade/calling/employment tax।

27

Budget 2026-27: मुख्य आंकड़े

BE 2026-27 में total expenditure लगभग ₹53.47 lakh crore; non-debt receipts ₹36.5 lakh crore; net tax receipts लगभग ₹28.7 lakh crore; gross tax revenue ₹44.04 lakh crore; revenue receipts ₹35.33 lakh crore; capital expenditure ₹12.22 lakh crore; effective capital expenditure ₹17.15 lakh crore; revenue expenditure ₹41.25 lakh crore।

28

Budget 2026-27: Deficit और Borrowing

Fiscal deficit target 4.3% GDP। Dated securities से net market borrowing ₹11.7 lakh crore; gross market borrowing ₹17.2 lakh crore; interest payments ₹14.04 lakh crore।

29

Budget 2026-27: Finance Commission

Budget 2026-27 Sixteenth Finance Commission award period का पहला वर्ष है। Divisible pool में States का share 41% बनाए रखने की recommendation सरकार ने स्वीकार की।

30

सरल Deficit Example

Revenue receipts ₹100, revenue expenditure ₹120, total expenditure ₹180 और borrowings छोड़कर receipts ₹140 हों: Revenue deficit ₹20; fiscal deficit ₹40; interest ₹15 होने पर primary deficit ₹25।

  • Expenditure: Revenue = recurring/maintenance; Capital = asset creation/acquisition या liability reduction।
